Ellis Information Quality Control Backend: Operations Manual

Version 3 September 2026 · Deliverable No. 10 · Maintained in step with the live system at ellis-visa.com

1. Access

Open ellis-visa.com and press "Quality console". No install, no account application. Test access is preconfigured.

The globe icon switches the interface and the data descriptions between English, Simplified Chinese and Traditional Chinese.

2. Records: the multi-dimensional spot check

The Records tab carries the full spot check. Filters combine freely:

FilterWhat it does
PassportThe station dimension. Type a country name or a code, for example China, CHN or CN. Chinese names work too.
DestinationSame input rules. Typing KOR or South Korea returns only South Korea records.
PurposeTourism, business, family visit, study, work, transit, other.
Visa requirementThe top-level class: visa-free, on arrival, required in advance, conditional.
ConfidenceHigh, medium, low.
Visa typeSubstring match on the product name, for example "tourist".
Passport typeThe 13 travel document types, from ordinary to diplomatic passports.
Missing fieldShows only records where a given field is still missing. Used for gap-filling work.

Four tiles follow the filter: record count, completeness (fillable and literal), source coverage and the confidence split.

Click a row to expand the full 25-field record. Every field carries a status:

fillednot publisheddoes not applymissing

The flag form on the card files a field-level issue into the correction queue.

3. Adding routes and editing fields

The "Add a route" card on the Records tab offers two ways in:

1Add with Ellis AI. Pick passport, destination, purpose and document type and press once. Ellis answers the route and checks it against official pages. A low-confidence answer is held until checked.
2Manual entry. Type the fields yourself. An official source URL and a short note are required. The record serves at once, with "Trip.com operations" as its provenance.

Every expanded record has three buttons:

Edit fieldsopens a prefilled form for every editable field and writes only what changed.
Refresh from sourcere-reads the official page now. It says plainly when nothing changed, and otherwise corrects the record and logs the quote.
Flagfiles a report. Ellis reads the page and places a proposal in the queue, which an operator accepts or declines in one click.

4. Correction queue: the five-stage loop

Every issue walks the five stages of standard 4.1.2, one button per stage, in order, with no skipping:

1Open. A reader or an operator flags a field on a record.
2Acknowledged. The information provider has been told.
3Corrected. The fix is written and the old answer is withdrawn from service at once.
4Reviewed. A second operator reviews. The system enforces that the reviewer is not the corrector.
5Published. The corrected answer goes live for readers.

Dismissal needs a written reason. The end-to-end target is 48 hours and every stage is timestamped and exportable.

5. Change log: add, modify, delete

Every change readers can see writes an entry classed add, modify or delete, with before and after values. Search covers the full history.

Each entry is also pushed as JSON to a webhook URL when one is configured, so a Slack, Teams or Feishu channel or an email bridge hears about it at once.

6. AI Q&A: sampling the assistant

The AI Q&A tab lists every exchange served: question, route read, answer and source. Each takes a ruling:

correct records the reviewer and the time.
wrong requires a written reason and automatically files a tracked issue on the answered route, which then walks the full correction loop.

7. Freshness: the automatic re-check

The sweep runs every six hours and re-reads every record whose last check is more than 40 hours old, oldest and never-checked first, so no record passes 48 hours unchecked. Any record can also be re-read on demand with Refresh from source.

A page that agrees restamps the record. A page that differs from a machine-held value corrects it, with the quote saved. A page that differs from a human-verified fact files a dispute for a person and changes nothing.

The Freshness tab shows the countdown to the next run, read from the scheduler itself, and the share of records attempted in the last 48 hours. A site that blocks robots counts as attempted, keeps its last good read, and is listed for a person.

8. Batch export

"Export Excel" on the Records tab exports an .xlsx following the active filters: a data sheet (25 fields, sources, confidence) and a field description sheet stamped with the UTC snapshot time. No row cap. A 12,000-row workbook builds in 1.2 seconds.

9. Holds and release

A Low answer (standard 4.2.3) is never shown. Readers see "We are checking this route". It is released by writing a fact verified against an official source, or by an audited operator release.

10. Deep links

Every route has a stable link such as #database/CHN/JPN/tourism that opens the record page.
