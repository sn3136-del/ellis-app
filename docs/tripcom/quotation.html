One monthly review re-reads every cached answer against its official government page. On the last run, 702 answers were re-read: most were confirmed by machine with no human time, 88 needed a person to rule on a discrepancy, and 153 needed a source link repaired.

This is an AI engineer, not a data clerk. The job is adjudicating what the machine flags against official sources, keeping the verification pipeline running, and holding the 48-hour correction and 24-hour urgent windows. Without that person the database drifts and the accuracy targets in section 6.1 stop being met. The deep-research runs in the table above are the same work on the machine side: each one reads up to twelve government pages for a route nobody has answered before.
