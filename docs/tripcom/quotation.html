5 · Why the build is $6,400 above your estimate

Your section 2.1 prices two things: setting the platform up, and rolling a version out to each site. Neither covers the acceptance programme your Delivery Standard makes mandatory. Section 5.3 requires, at every one of the five stages, three separate documents:
  • a test report for that stage
  • an acceptance checklist covering both function and data sampling
  • an acceptance report counter-signed by both parties
That is fifteen documents, plus the requirement-to-function matrix, the backend operating manual and the data-caliber manual. Writing them, running each stage and answering your findings is real work, so it is priced as its own line at $6,400 rather than hidden inside the per-site rate.

5 · 建设费为何比贵司测算高 6,400 美元

贵司 2.1 节计价的是两件事：把平台搭起来，以及把版本上线到每个站点。两者都不包含贵司交付标准强制要求的验收工作。§5.3 要求五个阶段中的每一个都必须提供三份独立文档：
  • 该阶段的测试报告
  • 涵盖功能与数据抽样的验收清单
  • 双方签署的验收报告
即 15 份文档，另加需求—功能对照表、后台操作说明与数据口径说明。编写这些文档、逐阶段执行验收并回应贵司的意见是实实在在的工作量，因此单列为 6,400 美元一项，而不是隐藏在站点单价里。
