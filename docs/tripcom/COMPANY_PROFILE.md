## Who we are
We are a software company built around one product: Ellis, a visa
information platform whose answers are bound to official government
sources. The company is founder-led and engineering-first; the founder
works directly with client teams, which is why iteration on Trip.com
feedback has been measured in days, not quarters.

## The product
Ellis answers visa questions the way an operations team needs them
answered: instantly, in the reader's language, and with the official
source attached. Under the surface it is three layers that keep each
other honest:

1. An AI engine that gives every route an immediate structured answer.
2. A human verification layer: corrections checked against named
   government pages, each carrying the source URL, the date and a
   verbatim quote. These outrank the engine wherever policy has moved.
3. A self-renewal pipeline that re-checks every served answer against its
   official page on a rolling schedule, compares fees, validity and stay
   figures digit by digit, and routes conflicts to a human queue. The
   machine never silently overrides a human check.
