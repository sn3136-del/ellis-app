Requirement ledger

Every requirement in the three Trip.com documents, checked against the running system rather than against intent. Seven auditors measured production independently, a challenger tried to refute every pass, and a critic looked for what nobody had checked. The failures below are stated because they are true, not because they are comfortable.

Measured 2026-08-30 against ellis-visa.com · 1,118 records · 187 destinations

Ellis does not pass §6.2 today.

The standard fails an acceptance on any single quantitative miss. Two miss. Field completeness is 98.99% against a 99% bar on the denominator Ellis argues for, and 91.48% on the literal reading of the clause. Monthly availability is 99.889% against 99.99%, and all three failed probes were caused by our own deployments today. A third, information accuracy, has never been formally measured by either party.

9. What it would take to pass

In the order that closes the acceptance fastest.

  • Field completeness. 209 cells stand between 98.99% and the bar, concentrated in Sudan, North Korea, Syria, Chad, Congo and Niger. Each needs a government page that publishes a fee or a validity, or a verified finding that none does. Days, not weeks.
  • Availability. Nothing to build. The metric needs a calendar month without a self-inflicted outage, and deployments must stop happening against the measured instance.
  • The classification tree. Fold Conditional into the three-value enum, or get Trip.com to accept a fourth value, and repair the 67 records whose subcategory does not nest under their primary. This one is visible to any reviewer who opens two records.
  • Station depth. Sixteen stations at roughly 14 destinations each need to reach the depth Hong Kong and the United States already have. This is the largest remaining body of work and it is the one Stage 4 is about.
  • The console's missing buttons, the departure city that changes nothing, the deep link that does not re-render, and the held page that hides its own switchers. Each is small and each is visible in a demo.

10. Questions only Trip.com can settle

  • Which completeness denominator governs: all required cells, or only those that can be filled given that a visa-free route has no visa validity and §4.2.1 forbids inventing what is unpublished?
  • Is Conditional acceptable as a fourth primary classification, or must electronic authorizations sit under 需提前办理?
  • For info_validity, do you want the policy's own expiry date, left blank where no government publishes one, or the date Ellis warrants the row to?
  • Does station coverage mean eighteen stations present, or eighteen at comparable depth? The answer changes the remaining scope by an order of magnitude.
  • Who performs the §4.2.2 accuracy sampling, and against which sample frame?
